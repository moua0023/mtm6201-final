ABOUT US

At ACT, we believe that active living is the foundation of a happy, connected community. Located in the heart of Ottawa, our state-of-the-art facility is built to welcome everyone—from toddlers taking their first steps on the ice, to seniors staying strong and mobile.

We are dedicated to providing accessible, high-quality sports, aquatics, and wellness programs under one roof. Whether you're training hard, learning a new sport, or gathering with neighbors, you belong here.

OUR MISSION

We bring Ottawa together through inclusive sports, state-of-the-art facilities, and vibrant youth programs.
